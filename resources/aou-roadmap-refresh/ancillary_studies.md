# Ancillary (partnered) studies: goals and emerging-workspace signal

> **Added 2026-10-14.** This brief documents the goals of the All of Us **partnered
> research studies** (a.k.a. "ancillary studies") and quantifies their footprint in
> the refreshed workspace registry (32,922 workspaces pulled 2026-10-14). These
> studies add *new data modalities* on top of the core protocol; several post-date
> the original 2020 Scientific Roadmap and some have data that is **only now being
> released or not yet released**, so they are under-represented in the publication
> corpus and must be carried in the roadmap refresh on the strength of their
> **stated goals** and their **workspace activity**, not their (still-thin) literature.

## Why they belong in every roadmap deliverable

The publication corpus is a **lagging** indicator: it reflects data released a year
or more ago. Ancillary studies are the program's **leading** edge — new modalities
(objective cognition/behavior, retinal imaging, precision-nutrition phenotyping,
exposomics) that will define the next wave of science. A roadmap that scores
opportunity only on existing publications would systematically **undervalue** them.
We therefore represent each study by its **goal** and its **workspace footprint**
(researchers' self-described intent), which leads publications.

## The current slate (goals verified against the official NIH page, reviewed 2026-07-16)

Source: NIH All of Us, *Partnered Research Studies*,
<https://www.nih.gov/allofus/funding/partnered-research-studies> (verified 2026-10-14).

| Study (sponsor) | Stated goal / purpose | New modality added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exploring the Mind** (NIMH) | "The goal of the project is to add **objective behavioral health data** to the All of Us database." Participants complete short exercises/puzzles measuring attention span, decision-making, response time and accuracy, and the ability to recognize emotions — helping researchers connect health factors and behavior. | Objective cognitive/behavioral task performance |
| **Eyes on Health** (NEI + NIBIB) | Collect eye (retina) images from **5,000** participants to explore how the eye connects to cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and neurological disorders — advancing **oculomics** ("the eye as a window to overall health") for early disease detection and personalized care. | Retinal imaging |
| **Nutrition for Precision Health (NPH)** (NIH Common Fund) | Discover how genes, culture, and environments affect how people respond to food; enroll **8,000** participants near study sites to share diet information, complete in-person health tests, and provide samples; develop **algorithms predicting individual responses to food and dietary patterns**. | Deep dietary phenotyping, metabolomics, CGM/in-person measures |
| **Environmental Health and Exposomics** (NIEHS) | Test **5,500+** stored blood samples to assess environmental exposures and their interactions with genomic and behavioral health factors, with a deeper investigation in participants with type 2 diabetes. | Exposomics / environmental biomarkers |
| *Antibody Testing for COVID-19* (NCI) — **completed** | Analyzed 24,000+ stored blood samples to study early U.S. spread of SARS-CoV-2 (found infections in five states earlier than first reported). | (completed; serology) |

*WEAR / Fitbit wearables are treated as a **core** program modality (captured under
the "Wearables & digital health" theme), not a partnered study, and are not listed
on the NIH partnered-studies page.*

## The headline finding (fold into deck + executive summary)

**Workspace activity precedes publications by a year or more.** For Exploring the
Mind there are **0** publications or preprints anywhere (verified live against
Europe PMC, which indexes preprints), yet **32 workspaces** already reference it —
**27 of them invisible in the pre-refresh workspace data.** This is the single
clearest demonstration of why the roadmap refresh must (a) pull **live** workspace
data, not a cached snapshot, and (b) score ancillary-study opportunity on **goals +
workspace intent**, not on a publication record that cannot yet exist. Mental-health
/ cognition work overall is now **13% of all workspaces** (4,293 / 32,922).

## Recommendation for the roadmap

- Give each ongoing ancillary study an explicit place in the relevant focus area
  (EtM → Mental health / behavioral & FA for brain/behavior; Eyes on Health →
  sensory/oculomics within FA for common conditions; NPH → lifestyle/nutrition;
  Exposomics → Environment), scored on **goal + workspace signal**, flagged
  "data emerging / not yet fully released."
- Add a standing **AoU-linked preprint stream** (Europe PMC `"All of Us" AND
  SRC:PPR`) to the corpus so emerging ancillary work surfaces at the preprint stage
  rather than waiting for indexed journal articles. (Would not add EtM *today* — no
  EtM preprints exist yet — but will catch the next wave earlier.)
