## Databases, tooling & agentic workflows

- **Jordan:** integrating existing databases (e.g., **drug mechanism of action**) could, in an agentic way, help researchers **prioritize or follow up findings**. Also the possibility that an **ancillary study could generate new data** (e.g., iPSCs — are they covered in our consent?). Low-hanging fruit: the highest-quality biomedical databases would be really useful. Example: to ask whether a drug is **repurposable for depression**, you'd want a list of candidates to mount a **target-trial emulation** of drugs corresponding to a mechanism of action; or, on finding a variant, do **variant-to-function mapping**. Normally people take data *outside* AoU and run it through their favorite tool.
- **Melissa:** consider making some **tooling recommendations**. **→ ACTION (Melissa):** draft tooling recommendations.
