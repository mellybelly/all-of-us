# Resources

Background materials for the All of Us Research Program science-committee strategic-insights project (next five years).

## Sources

### Original PMI recommendations (foundational)

- **ACD Working Group on the Precision Medicine Initiative — landing page.** Background, charge, roster.
  - Live: https://acd.od.nih.gov/working-groups/pmi.html
  - Archived (Wayback, 2024): https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://acd.od.nih.gov/working-groups/pmi.html
- **PMI Working Group final report to the ACD (September 17, 2015).** Local copy: [`PMI_WG_report_2015-09-17-Final.pdf`](PMI_WG_report_2015-09-17-Final.pdf)
  - Original: https://acd.od.nih.gov/documents/reports/PMI_WG_report_2015-09-17-Final.pdf
- **ACD presentation deck (September 17, 2015).** Local copy: [`09172015-PMI-ACD-presentation.pdf`](09172015-PMI-ACD-presentation.pdf)
  - Original: https://acd.od.nih.gov/documents/presentations/09172015-PMI.pdf

Context: President Obama launched the PMI on January 20, 2015. The ACD working group was charged with developing the vision and design for a research cohort of one million or more participants — the program that became *All of Us*. The September 2015 report is the foundational recommendation document.
