# disease-burden — burden & funding-alignment layer

**Added 2026-06-11** per the June 3, 2026 WG meeting (JMV requested disease
burden; Melissa committed to adding it "along with PubMed trends"). See
`../wg-meeting-notes/2026-06-03_second-meeting.md`.

## What this answers

Whether AoU's research attention is pointed at the conditions that carry the
most US population burden — and, where it isn't, whether that is a gap the
Scientific Roadmap Refresh should name. Overlays disease burden (GBD US) and
NIH funding-alignment (RCDC + literature) onto the existing 19-theme /
8-focus-area corpus map.

## Headline findings

- **NIH funding tracks disease burden weakly** (R² < 0.03 excl. HIV; only ~0.31%
  funding rise per 1% DALY rise) — so AoU's value is to be a burden-*corrective*,
  not a burden-mirror.
- **Musculoskeletal / low back pain — the #1 US cause of disability — has no
  theme in the AoU taxonomy.** The single clearest burden-attention gap.
- **Aging/ADRD, respiratory/sleep, and neurology are high-burden but
  under-indexed**; **dermatology is low-burden but over-indexed** (reconfirms
  the known artifact).
- **Long COVID** illustrates AoU's potential as an early-warning instrument for
  burden that lags NIH funding categorization.

## Method & honesty note

Burden tiers are **analyst judgment anchored to published GBD 2021 US
leading-cause rankings — not invented DALY point-estimates**. The exact
RCDC-dollars × GBD-DALYs × AoU-theme-counts join is a flagged follow-on; the
data sources for it are in `sources.md` (entries 4 and 7).
