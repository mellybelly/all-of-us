## Mission

NIEHS leads environmental health science research. The FY2025–2029 Strategic Plan (final draft) covers foundational environmental health science, climate & health, environmental justice, exposome/exposure science, New Approach Methodologies (NAMs), and workforce/infrastructure.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Advance environmental health science** (NIEHS-T1). Foundational and translational science on how environmental exposures affect human health across the lifespan.
- **Climate and human health** (NIEHS-T2). Understand and address climate-change impacts on health, including extreme heat, air quality, wildfire, water.
- **Environmental justice & community-engaged research** (NIEHS-T3). Promote environmental justice through community-engaged research and reducing exposure disparities.
- **Exposome and exposure science** (NIEHS-T4). Characterize the exposome (cumulative environmental exposures) and integrate with genomics.
- **New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)** (NIEHS-T5). Develop and validate NAMs (alternatives to animal models) for toxicology and risk assessment.
- **Workforce & data infrastructure** (NIEHS-T6). Build environmental-health workforce and data/standards infrastructure.
