# NIAID — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIAID funds research on infectious, immune-mediated, and allergic diseases. The FY2026 Congressional Justification reaffirms priorities in emerging infectious diseases, HIV/AIDS, TB, antimicrobial resistance, fundamental immunology, autoimmune disease, allergy/asthma, and transplantation.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Emerging and re-emerging infectious diseases** (NIAID-EID). Maintain pandemic preparedness: surveillance, diagnostics, therapeutics, vaccines for emerging pathogens.
- **HIV/AIDS research** (NIAID-HIV). Cure-directed HIV research and prevention including PrEP/long-acting agents and global HIV control.
- **Tuberculosis** (NIAID-TB). TB diagnostics, drug discovery, vaccine development.
- **Antimicrobial resistance** (NIAID-AB-RES). Combat antimicrobial resistance via diagnostics, novel agents, stewardship research.
- **Immunology fundamentals** (NIAID-IMM). Foundational immunology including immune-mediated disease mechanisms.
- **Autoimmune & immune-mediated disease** (NIAID-AUTO). Mechanisms and therapies for autoimmune and immune-mediated conditions (lupus, RA, IBD, psoriasis, etc.).
- **Allergy & asthma** (NIAID-ALL). Allergic and asthmatic disease mechanisms and therapies.
- **Transplantation** (NIAID-TX). Transplant immunology and immune tolerance research.
