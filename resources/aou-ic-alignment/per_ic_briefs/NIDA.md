# NIDA — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIDA funds research on the causes, consequences, prevention, and treatment of drug use and substance use disorders. The 2022–2026 plan's thematic areas are prevention, treatment, public health (including HIV), neurobiology of addiction, disparities, workforce, and emerging issues (polysubstance, cannabis, e-cigarettes, fentanyl).

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Prevention of substance use** (NIDA-G1). Research to prevent initiation of substance use and progression to substance use disorder.
- **Treatment of substance use disorders** (NIDA-G2). Medications, behavioral interventions, and harm-reduction approaches for substance use disorders.
- **Public health implications: HIV, hepatitis, harms** (NIDA-G3). Address the public-health consequences of substance use including HIV, HCV, opioid overdose.
- **Neurobiology of addiction** (NIDA-G4). Foundational neurobiology of reward, motivation, and addiction.
- **Health disparities and equity in SUD** (NIDA-G5). Address racial/ethnic, socioeconomic, and geographic disparities in SUD prevention, treatment, and outcomes.
- **Cross-cutting: workforce, training, dissemination** (NIDA-G6). Build the addiction-research workforce; dissemination and implementation.
- **Polysubstance / cannabis / e-cigarette emerging issues** (NIDA-G7). Address emerging public health concerns: polysubstance use, cannabis, e-cigarettes, fentanyl.
