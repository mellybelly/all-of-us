# NCI — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

The National Cancer Institute leads, conducts, and supports cancer research, training, dissemination, and access programs across the National Cancer Program. Its strategic frame is the 2023 National Cancer Plan (8 goals to end cancer as we know it) and the Annual Plan & Budget Proposal that translates the plan into funding priorities.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Prevent Cancer** (NCP-G1). All people and society adopt proven strategies that reduce the risk of cancer.
- **Detect Cancers Early** (NCP-G2). Cancers are detected and treated at early stages, enabling more effective treatment and reducing mortality.
- **Develop Effective Treatments** (NCP-G3). Effective treatment, with minimal side effects, is accessible to all people including rare and metastatic cancers.
- **Eliminate Inequities** (NCP-G4). Disparities in cancer risk, incidence, treatment, and mortality are eliminated through equitable access.
- **Deliver Optimal Care** (NCP-G5). Health care system delivers evidence-based, patient-centered care prioritizing prevention and survivorship.
- **Engage Every Person** (NCP-G6). Every person with cancer or at risk has opportunity to participate in research; barriers eliminated.
- **Maximize Data Utility** (NCP-G7). Secure sharing of privacy-protected health data is standard practice across research.
- **Optimize the Workforce** (NCP-G8). Cancer care and research workforce is diverse, reflects communities served, meets needs of all people.
- **Strengthening the future cancer workforce** (NCIB-WORKFORCE). Support training pipeline (K99/R00, ESI payline) and emerging data-science / public-health skills.
- **Modernizing cancer clinical studies** (NCIB-MODERNIZE). Modernize clinical research with accessible, inclusive, nimble studies including the Cancer Screening Research Network (CSRN, multi-cancer detection).
- **Childhood Cancer Data Initiative** (NCIB-CHILDHOOD). Dedicated $50M for CCDI to expand pediatric cancer data infrastructure.
- **AI for screening and precision oncology** (NCIB-AI). Leverage AI to predict treatments, enhance precancer screening, and detect hardest-to-detect cancers.
- **Sustained foundational cancer biology research** (NCIB-FOUNDATION). Continue to support basic cancer biology as upstream input for prevention, detection, treatment.
- **Reach populations disproportionately affected** (NCIB-ACCESS). Empower the workforce to reach people affected disproportionately and address access barriers to care.
