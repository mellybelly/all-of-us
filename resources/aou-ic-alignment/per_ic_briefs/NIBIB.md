# NIBIB — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIBIB funds biomedical engineering, imaging, and bioengineering research. Its strategic plan's four goals cover innovative biomedical technologies, point-of-care/wireless/personal health informatics, mechanism-to-clinical translation, and biomedical imaging.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Innovative biomedical technologies** (NIBIB-G1). Develop innovative biomedical technologies that integrate engineering with the physical and life sciences to solve complex problems and improve health.
- **Point-of-care, wireless, personal health informatics** (NIBIB-G2). Enable patient-centered health care through development of point-of-care, wireless, and personal health informatics technologies.
- **Translate advances in mechanisms** (NIBIB-G3). Transform advances in knowledge of cellular and molecular disease mechanisms into clinical applications.
- **Biomedical imaging** (NIBIB-G4). Drive biomedical imaging research from basic discovery to clinical translation.
