# NIDDK — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIDDK supports research on diabetes, digestive, kidney, and hematologic diseases; obesity; and nutrition. Its 2025 plan has four Scientific Goals (foundational, clinical trials, dissemination, stakeholder engagement) and detailed Research Opportunities under each.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Biological pathways & environmental contributors** (NIDDK-G1-RO1). Identify and characterize factors that affect human health (genetic, molecular, environmental).
- **Biology × behavior × environment links** (NIDDK-G1-RO2). Analyze links between biology, behavior, and aspects of the environment.
- **Innovative technologies & data science** (NIDDK-G1-RO3). Develop innovative technologies and resources and expand data science to advance scientific progress and enhance health.
- **Pivotal clinical studies and trials** (NIDDK-G2). Advance pivotal clinical studies and trials for prevention, treatment, and cures.
- **Dissemination & implementation** (NIDDK-G3). Advance research to disseminate and implement evidence-based prevention strategies and treatments in clinics and community settings.
- **Stakeholder & participant engagement** (NIDDK-G4). Advance stakeholder engagement — including patients and other participants as true partners in research.
- **Diabetes (T1D, T2D, gestational, obesity-driven)** (NIDDK-DIAB). Major NIDDK research portfolio: type 1, type 2, and gestational diabetes; obesity and insulin resistance.
- **Chronic kidney disease & urology** (NIDDK-CKD). Kidney and urologic diseases — CKD, ESRD, urologic conditions across the lifespan.
- **Digestive & liver diseases (incl. MASLD/IBD)** (NIDDK-DIG). Digestive diseases including IBD, MASLD/MASH, liver disease, and microbiome.
- **Obesity & metabolic health** (NIDDK-OBES). Obesity affects >40% U.S. adults; GLP-1/SGLT2 therapy, weight regulation, prevention.
- **Gut microbiome** (NIDDK-MICRO). Role of the human microbiome in IBD and other diseases.
