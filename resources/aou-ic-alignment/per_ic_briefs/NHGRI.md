# NHGRI — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NHGRI is NIH's institute for human genomics, with the 2020 Strategic Vision (Nature, 'Forefront of Genomics') organized as four areas (principles & values; robust foundation; breaking down barriers; compelling research projects) and ten Bold Predictions for human genomics by 2030.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Robust foundation for genomics** (NHGRI-A1). Build/maintain genomics tools, technologies, datasets, and resources for the entire biomedical community.
- **Breaking down barriers** (NHGRI-A2). Eliminate barriers between research and clinical care, between fields, and between participants and benefits.
- **Compelling genomics research projects** (NHGRI-A3). Drive ambitious flagship projects that advance the science of genomics including non-coding variation, multi-ancestry inclusion, and clinical implementation.
- **Guiding principles & values (incl. ELSI)** (NHGRI-A4). Foreground genomic ethical/legal/social implications research, ancestry inclusivity, and societal stewardship.
- **Move beyond race-based population descriptors** (NHGRI-BP4). Bold prediction: 'Research in human genomics will have moved beyond population descriptors based on historic social constructs such as race.'
- **Make genomic information routine in clinical care** (NHGRI-BP6). Bold prediction: 'genomic information will have transitioned from boutique to mainstream in all clinical settings'.
- **Eliminate variants of uncertain significance** (NHGRI-BP7). Bold prediction: 'the clinical relevance of all encountered genomic variants will be readily predictable, rendering VUS obsolete.'
- **Equitable benefit from genomics for ancestrally diverse populations** (NHGRI-BP9). Bold prediction: 'Individuals from ancestrally diverse backgrounds will benefit equitably from advances in human genomics.'
- **Curative genomic therapies for genetic diseases** (NHGRI-BP10). Bold prediction: 'Breakthrough discoveries will lead to curative therapies involving genomic modifications for dozens of genetic diseases.'
- **Functional annotation of every human gene** (NHGRI-BP2). Bold prediction: 'The biological function(s) of every human gene will be known.'
