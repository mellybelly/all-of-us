# NHLBI — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NHLBI funds heart, lung, blood, and sleep (HLBS) research. Its FY2026–2030 Strategic Vision sets four goals, eight objectives, and five crosscutting themes (data science, health disparities, lifestyle, community engagement, women's health).

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Understand normal biological function and resilience** (NHLBI-O1). Foundational research on healthy heart/lung/blood/sleep biology and resilience mechanisms.
- **Investigate pathobiological mechanisms of HLBS disease** (NHLBI-O2). Investigate newly discovered pathobiological mechanisms important to onset and progression of heart, lung, and blood diseases and sleep disorders.
- **Differences in health status among populations** (NHLBI-O3). Investigate factors that account for differences in health status among populations.
- **Individual differences in pathobiology and treatment response** (NHLBI-O4). Identify factors that account for individual differences in pathobiology and responses to treatments.
- **Novel diagnostic and therapeutic strategies** (NHLBI-O5). Develop and optimize novel diagnostic and therapeutic strategies to prevent, treat, and cure HLBS diseases.
- **Clinical and implementation research** (NHLBI-O6). Optimize clinical and implementation research to improve health and reduce disease.
- **Data science frontiers in HLBS** (NHLBI-O7). Leverage emerging opportunities in data science to open new frontiers in HLBS research.
- **Workforce for HLBS science** (NHLBI-O8). Develop and sustain a scientific workforce capable of accomplishing the NHLBI's mission.
- **Crosscutting: Data Science** (NHLBI-CCT-DS). Data science crosscuts all eight objectives — foundation models, multi-omics, AI/ML on HLBS data.
- **Crosscutting: Health Disparities** (NHLBI-CCT-HD). Health disparities crosscut all eight objectives.
- **Crosscutting: Lifestyle Behaviors** (NHLBI-CCT-LIFE). Lifestyle behaviors (physical activity, smoking, diet, sleep) crosscut HLBS science.
- **Crosscutting: Community & Patient-Engaged Research** (NHLBI-CCT-CE). Community and patient-engaged research crosscut HLBS objectives.
- **Crosscutting: Women's Health** (NHLBI-CCT-WH). Women's health crosscuts all eight objectives.
