# NIH-Wide — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIH is the largest public funder of biomedical research worldwide, organized as 27 Institutes and Centers under a 2021–2025 Strategic Plan with three Objectives (advance biomedical and behavioral sciences; develop and sustain scientific capacity; ensure integrity and accountability) and five crosscutting themes (minority health, women's health, lifespan, collaboration, data science).

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Driving Foundational Science** (NIHW-OBJ1-A). Advance basic, translational, clinical, and population-based science across biology and behavior, including building data resources and tools.
- **Preventing Disease and Promoting Health** (NIHW-OBJ1-B). Prevent disease and promote health through population-based science, behavioral/social determinants research, and lifestyle interventions.
- **Advancing Treatments and Cures** (NIHW-OBJ1-C). Catalyze translation of discoveries into treatments and cures via partnerships with industry, patients, and other stakeholders.
- **Enhancing Workforce** (NIHW-OBJ2-A). Develop and maintain a diverse biomedical and behavioral research workforce.
- **Supporting Research Resources and Infrastructure** (NIHW-OBJ2-B). Sustain shared infrastructure including data resources, cohorts, and informatics, supporting research at scale.
- **Scientific Integrity, Stewardship & Social Responsibility** (NIHW-OBJ3). Promote highest level of scientific integrity, public accountability, and social responsibility in conduct of science.
- **Improving Minority Health & Reducing Health Disparities** (NIHW-CCT1). Pan-NIH crosscutting theme: address structural inequities and improve minority health.
- **Enhancing Women's Health** (NIHW-CCT2). Pan-NIH crosscutting theme: women's health beyond reproductive health to address conditions affecting women differently.
- **Addressing Public Health Challenges Across the Lifespan** (NIHW-CCT3). Pan-NIH crosscutting theme: lifespan health, with attention to maternal, child, adolescent, adult, and older-adult priorities.
- **Promoting Collaborative Science** (NIHW-CCT4). Pan-NIH crosscutting theme: team science and trans-NIH collaboration.
- **Leveraging Data Science for Biomedical Discovery** (NIHW-CCT5). Pan-NIH crosscutting theme: data science and AI/ML enablement across the agency.
