# NIMH — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIMH funds research on mental illnesses. The 2023 Strategic Plan Update has four goals (brain mechanisms; lifespan; interventions; public health impact) with cross-cutting themes including comorbidities, computational approaches, harnessing data, and suicide prevention.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Brain mechanisms underlying complex behaviors** (NIMH-G1). Define the brain mechanisms underlying complex behaviors (cognitive, affective, social processes; neural circuits).
- **Genomic & non-genomic factors in mental illness** (NIMH-G1-O2). Identify genomic and non-genomic factors associated with mental illnesses.
- **Examine mental illnesses across the lifespan** (NIMH-G2). Characterize trajectories of mental illness, neurodevelopment, and aging.
- **Effective interventions & implementation** (NIMH-G3). Develop and test interventions; advance implementation in community and clinical settings.
- **Strengthen public health impact** (NIMH-G4). Strengthen the public-health impact of NIMH-supported research; reduce mental health disparities.
- **Comorbidities of mental and physical disorders** (NIMH-X-COMOR). Comorbidities cross-cutting theme: integrate mental and physical health.
- **Harnessing the power of data** (NIMH-X-DATA). Cross-cutting theme: data resources, computational approaches, EHR/genomic integration.
- **Suicide prevention research** (NIMH-X-SUICIDE). Cross-cutting NIMH priority: suicide prevention research.
- **Long COVID & psychiatric sequelae** (NIMH-X-LONG-COVID-MH). Emerging priority: psychiatric and cognitive sequelae of COVID-19.
