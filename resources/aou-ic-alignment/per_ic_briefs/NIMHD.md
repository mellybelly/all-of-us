# NIMHD — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIMHD leads research on minority health and health disparities. The 2021–2025 plan has nine goals organized in three categories (scientific research, research sustaining, outreach), plus a Leap Forward Research Challenge for visionary cross-NIH disparities science.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Promote research on health-disparity populations** (NIMHD-G1). Understand and improve the health of populations experiencing health disparities.
- **Causes of health disparities** (NIMHD-G2). Advance scientific understanding of the causes of health disparities through basic, behavioral, clinical, applied research.
- **Develop and test interventions** (NIMHD-G3). Develop and test interventions to reduce health disparities.
- **Methods, metrics, measures, tools** (NIMHD-G4). Create and improve scientific methods, metrics, measures, and tools that support health-disparities research.
- **Disparities-researcher training** (NIMHD-G5). Support training and career progression of health-disparities researchers.
- **National capacity to conduct HD research** (NIMHD-G6). Strengthen national capacity to conduct health-disparities research.
- **Ensure appropriate participation** (NIMHD-G7). Ensure participation in NIH-funded research is scientifically appropriate; address inclusion in big-data initiatives.
- **Community engagement & dissemination** (NIMHD-G8). Promote evidence-based community engagement, dissemination of findings.
- **Cross-sector partnerships** (NIMHD-G9). Establish partnerships with nongovernmental groups to advance HD definitions and best practices.
- **Leap Forward Research Challenge** (NIMHD-LEAP). Visionary cross-NIH priorities including geo-coded longitudinal cohorts, multi-omics in HD populations, structural-racism mechanisms.
