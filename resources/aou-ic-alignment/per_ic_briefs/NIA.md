# NIA — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIA leads NIH research on aging and is the designated lead Institute for Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. Its 2020–2025 Strategic Directions are organized as Goals A–I across aging biology, dementia, social factors, disparities, infrastructure, and dissemination.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Biology of aging** (NIA-A). Better understand the biology of aging and its impact on the prevention, progression, and prognosis of disease and disability.
- **Geroscience: connect biology and chronic disease** (NIA-B). Apply biology of aging to chronic diseases of older adults (geroscience).
- **Healthy aging, behavioral & social factors** (NIA-C). Improve understanding of the effects of personal, interpersonal, and societal factors on aging.
- **Aging brain & Alzheimer's/ADRD** (NIA-D). Improve understanding of the aging brain, AD, related dementias, and other neurodegenerative diseases; develop interventions.
- **Aging-society consequences** (NIA-E). Improve understanding of consequences of an aging society to inform intervention development and policy.
- **Aging health disparities** (NIA-F). Understand health disparities related to aging and develop strategies to improve health status in diverse older adults.
- **Research infrastructure** (NIA-G). Support infrastructure and resources for high-quality aging research.
- **Dissemination of aging-research findings** (NIA-H). Disseminate research findings to public, scientific communities, policymakers, and stakeholders.
