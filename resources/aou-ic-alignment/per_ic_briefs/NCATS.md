# NCATS — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NCATS funds translational science research and supports the CTSA Program. The 2025–2030 plan covers therapeutic & diagnostic development, the CTSA Program, rare diseases (RDCRN/TRND/repurposing), crosscutting strategies (team science, data science, partnerships), and stewardship.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Therapeutic & Diagnostic Development Across Diseases** (NCATS-G1). Advance therapy development for everyone by identifying biological, behavioral, environmental factors affecting health and disease.
- **Clinical Translational Science Awards (CTSA) Program** (NCATS-G2). Strengthen the CTSA Program as a national infrastructure for translational science.
- **Rare Diseases (RDCRN, etc.)** (NCATS-G3). Accelerate rare-disease research via RDCRN, Therapeutics for Rare and Neglected Diseases (TRND), repurposing programs.
- **Crosscutting translational strategies** (NCATS-G4). Leverage crosscutting strategies (team science, partnerships, data science, dissemination).
- **Stewardship, transparency, integrity** (NCATS-G5). Champion effective stewardship of translational science through transparency, integrity, accountability.
- **AI & data science in translational science** (NCATS-AI). Develop AI/ML for translational science applications including drug repurposing.
