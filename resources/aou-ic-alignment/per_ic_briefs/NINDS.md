# NINDS — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NINDS funds research on neurological disorders. The Interim 2025–2026 Strategic Plan covers foundational neuroscience, translational/clinical neurology, workforce, data science & cohort infrastructure, health equity, pain research (HEAL Initiative), and stroke.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Foundational neuroscience** (NINDS-G1). Support foundational neuroscience including basic mechanisms of brain function.
- **Translational and clinical neurology** (NINDS-G2). Translational and clinical research for neurological disorders (stroke, epilepsy, headache, PD, neuromuscular).
- **Workforce development** (NINDS-G3). Train next-generation neuroscientists across career stages.
- **Data science & cohort infrastructure** (NINDS-G4). Advance data science, large-cohort infrastructure (e.g., HEAL Data Ecosystem) for neurology research.
- **Health equity in neurology** (NINDS-G5). Address neurology disparities including stroke, dementia, and access to neurological care.
- **Pain & HEAL** (NINDS-PAIN). NIH HEAL Initiative pain-research portfolio (chronic pain, non-opioid analgesics).
- **Stroke prevention & treatment** (NINDS-STROKE). Reduce stroke incidence and improve outcomes including for diverse populations.
