# NLM — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NLM is the world's largest biomedical library and the locus for data science and open science at NIH. The 2017–2027 plan has three Goals (digital research enterprise; dissemination & engagement; workforce) with explicit mention of supporting All of Us, BRAIN Initiative, and Cancer Moonshot data.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Connect resources of a digital research enterprise** (NLM-G1-O1). Collect/curate/integrate digital research objects with FAIR principles across literature and data.
- **Advance research in biomedical informatics and data science** (NLM-G1-O2). Drive R&D in informatics: curation at scale, analytics, visualization, modeling, NLP.
- **Open science policies** (NLM-G1-O3). Develop and implement policies that facilitate open science and data-driven research.
- **Sustainable computational infrastructure** (NLM-G1-O4). Create sustainable institutional, physical, and computational infrastructure for data science at NIH.
- **Reach more people through dissemination & engagement** (NLM-G2). Enhance dissemination/engagement pathways across scholars, clinicians, communities.
- **Build a workforce for data-driven research and health** (NLM-G3). Train next-generation biomedical informatics and data-science workforce.
- **Engage with large NIH initiatives (All of Us, BRAIN, Cancer Moonshot)** (NLM-COHORTS). NLM will engage with the entire NIH to determine how to accommodate data generated in large initiatives such as the BRAIN Initiative, All of Us, and the Cancer Moonshot.
- **Data standards & curation at scale** (NLM-STANDARDS). Develop, house, support standards for terminologies, vocabularies, common data elements; curate at scale including for environmental, behavioral, interactional data.
