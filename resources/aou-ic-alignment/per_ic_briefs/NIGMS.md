# NIGMS — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NIGMS funds basic biomedical research not focused on a specific disease, including pharmacogenomics (PGRN), training, and capacity building (IDeA). The 2021–2025 plan has five goals around investigator-initiated research, diverse workforce, capacity building, scientific themes (including PGx), and stewardship.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Investigator-initiated foundational research** (NIGMS-G1). Sustain strong support for investigator-initiated research that produces foundational discoveries.
- **Workforce diversity & training** (NIGMS-G2). Build a robust and diverse scientific workforce through training and career development.
- **Capacity-building / IDeA / institutional development** (NIGMS-G3). Develop research capacity in underserved geographic regions (IDeA program).
- **Pharmacogenomics & precision medicine** (NIGMS-G4). Foundational pharmacogenomics research including PGRN (Pharmacogenomics Research Network).
- **Operations & stewardship** (NIGMS-G5). Steward NIGMS resources, partnerships, information systems, and trans-NIH collaboration.
