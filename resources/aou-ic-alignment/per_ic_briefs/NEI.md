# NEI — AoU Alignment Brief

## Mission

NEI funds vision research. The 2021–2025 plan covers visual neuroscience, retinal disease, glaucoma, regenerative medicine (Audacious Goals Initiative), vision-care disparities, data science & imaging, and workforce.

## Strategic-plan priorities

- **Visual neuroscience & retinal biology** (NEI-G1). Advance basic visual neuroscience and retinal biology.
- **Retinal diseases (AMD, diabetic retinopathy)** (NEI-G2). Advance prevention and treatment of retinal diseases — age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy.
- **Glaucoma** (NEI-G3). Glaucoma research: mechanisms, imaging, intervention.
- **Regenerative medicine & gene therapy for eye disease** (NEI-G4). Audacious Goal Initiative: regenerate neurons and neural connections in retina.
- **Health disparities in vision** (NEI-G5). Address racial/ethnic and socioeconomic disparities in eye health and access to vision care.
- **Data science & imaging for ophthalmology** (NEI-G6). Advance AI/ML and imaging research for ophthalmology, including teleophthalmology.
- **Workforce in vision research** (NEI-G7). Train next-generation vision researchers and clinician-scientists.
