# AoU × NIH-IC Alignment — Gap Analysis

**Phase 3c deliverable, AoU Scientific Roadmap Refresh evidence base.**
Inputs: 1,374 substantive AoU publications + 11,577 substantive workspace
projects (theme-tagged in Phase 2c); 22 NIH strategic-plan PDFs (in scope:
NIH-Wide + 21 ICs); 166 priorities extracted from those plans
(`ic_priorities.csv`); OD-administered pubs re-attributed to substantive-
science ICs via fractional theme→IC mapping (`theme_to_ic_mapping.csv`).
Method: see `README.md`.

Reading order: (A) alignment hits, (B) demand-side gaps (IC wants it,
AoU isn't producing it), (C) latent areas (AoU is producing, IC plans don't
emphasize), (D) pan-NIH crosscutting themes most relevant to AoU's
positioning as cross-IC infrastructure.

---

## (A) Where ICs prioritize what AoU is delivering — *alignment hits*

Pairs where the IC has explicit strategic-plan priorities for the theme
**and** AoU has substantial corpus activity (fractional-attributed pubs
+ project density). Organized by 2026 Sci Com WG topic where applicable.

### A1. Genetics / Genomics / Roadmap Refresh evidence

1. **NHGRI × Genomics methods (Theme 2).** Composite score is the
   single largest cell in the matrix (corpus 422; priorities 9: the
   "Forefront of Genomics" framework's 10 Bold Predictions map almost
   directly onto AoU strengths — multi-ancestry methods, PRS portability,
   long-read sequencing utility, ancestry-aware analytic methods).
   AoU is *the cohort* delivering NHGRI's Bold Prediction #9 (equitable
   benefit from genomics for ancestrally diverse populations).
   Refresh implication: this is the strongest "AoU delivers IC mission"
   story — lead with it.
2. **NHGRI × Rare disease (Theme 10).** NHGRI prioritizes VUS resolution
   (Bold Prediction #7) and curative genomic therapies (#10); AoU has
   54 rare-disease pubs and 433 projects, including penetrance/expressivity
   in unselected populations (a unique AoU contribution) and ACMG
   actionable-gene findings.
3. **NHGRI × ELSI (Theme 17).** NHGRI's "guiding principles & values"
   area maps cleanly onto AoU's ELSI portfolio (69 pubs). Although ELSI
   share is declining (Phase 3b trend −12.3), the work is still aligned
   with NHGRI's continuing ELSI-Research Program priority.

### A2. Common diseases / Roadmap Refresh evidence

4. **NHLBI × Cardiometabolic (Theme 1).** NHLBI's FY2026–2030 Strategic
   Vision puts cardiometabolic disease front-and-center across Objectives
   2/3/4/5 plus the Crosscutting Lifestyle theme; AoU produced 382 pubs
   and 3,292 projects in this area. Together this is the second-largest
   matrix cell.
5. **NHLBI × Respiratory & sleep (Theme 13).** NHLBI is the only IC home
   for OSA/asthma/COPD; AoU has 59 pubs, 405 projects, and HA2 wearables
   evidence (70 pubs) increasingly enables sleep-disorder phenotyping.
6. **NIDDK × Cardiometabolic (Theme 1).** NIDDK's 2025 plan covers diabetes
   (T1D/T2D/gestational), obesity, CKD, MASLD — all major AoU topic
   clusters. Combined NIDDK priority count: 11. Co-attribution with NHLBI
   reflects the CKM-syndrome reality.
7. **NCI × Cancer (Theme 5).** NCI National Cancer Plan's 8 goals + 6
   FY26 Annual Plan priorities. AoU has 176 pubs / 1,722 projects. NCI
   Goal 7 (Maximize Data Utility) maps directly to AoU as a data resource.

### A3. Behavioral / lifestyle / Roadmap Refresh evidence

8. **NIMH × Mental health (Theme 4).** NIMH's 4-goal plan + multiple
   cross-cutting themes (Comorbidities, Power of Data, Suicide
   Prevention). AoU produced 254 mental-health pubs / 1,937 projects
   in this area, the #4 largest theme. NIMH Goal 1 Objective 1.2 (genomic
   and non-genomic factors in mental illness) maps directly onto AoU's
   psychiatric-genetics work (e.g., PMID:40007508 ESR1 depression GWAS).
9. **NIDA × Substance use (Theme 4 subtheme).** AoU corpus tobacco/
   cannabis/opioid work (PMID:39231067) aligns with NIDA's Prevention
   and Treatment goals; AoU adds the longitudinal-EHR-with-SDOH
   dimension NIDA can't get from registries.
10. **NIAAA × Alcohol (Theme 4 subtheme).** NIAAA Goal 5 (alcohol-related
    disparities) maps onto AoU corpus AUD work; AoU is well-positioned
    for NIAAA Goal 2 (prevention research using national cohorts).

### A4. Equity / SDOH

11. **NIMHD × SDOH/Disparities (Theme 6).** Largest aligned cell in
    NIMHD's row. 9 priorities + 458 pubs / 3,070 projects in AoU
    (33.3% of corpus). NIMHD Goal 7 explicitly asks for "analysis of
    populations experiencing health disparities in big data sets,
    clinical research, and future big science initiatives" — that's
    literally the AoU mandate.
12. **NCI × Disparities (Theme 5+6).** NCI Goal 4 (Eliminate Inequities)
    + AoU's cancer-disparities portfolio.
13. **NEI × Sensory disparities (Theme 18).** NEI's Vision Health
    Equity priority + 107 AoU pubs on ophthalmology disparities
    (e.g., PMID:38200661 glaucoma access).

### A5. Aging / NIA

14. **NIA × Aging/ADRD (Theme 11).** NIA Goals A, D, F (biology of
    aging; AD/ADRD; aging disparities); AoU has 75 pubs / 637 projects,
    growing rapidly post-2023 (+4.7 share trend).

### A6. Infrastructure & data science / AI WG evidence

15. **NLM × Methods/Infrastructure (Theme 3).** NLM Goal 1 + the explicit
    NLM-AoU partnership statement ("NLM will engage with the entire NIH
    to determine how to accommodate data generated in ... All of Us").
    251 AoU methods pubs / 2,389 projects.
16. **NCATS × Rare disease (Theme 10).** NCATS plan Goal 3 (rare diseases
    via RDCRN/TRND/repurposing); AoU rare-disease portfolio (54 pubs)
    overlaps. NCATS Translator Program is a downstream Monarch/AoU data
    consumer.
17. **NCATS × Data science (Theme 3+10+16).** NCATS Goal 4 / Objective
    4-3 (data science strategies and standards). AoU is a natural
    NCATS Translator data source.

### A7. Devices / Imaging / Imaging WG evidence

18. **NIBIB × Wearables (Theme 7).** NIBIB Goal 2 (point-of-care,
    wireless, personal health informatics) maps onto AoU's 73 wearable
    pubs / 923 projects. Wearables is Roadmap FA-candidate territory.

### A8. Cross-cutting NIH-Wide alignment

19. **NIH-Wide × ELSI (Theme 17).** NIH-Wide Crosscutting Theme 5
    (Data Science) and Objective 1 (foundational science) explicitly
    name AoU-like infrastructure. AoU is the empirical test of the
    pan-NIH cohort-data-resource model.

### Headline reads for the Refresh

- *The genetics-and-genomics story is the strongest WG-1 (Roadmap Refresh)
  evidence anywhere in the matrix.* AoU + NHGRI is the highest-leverage
  alignment in the entire analysis. Lead with it.
- *Mental health alignment is the strongest behavioral evidence.* NIMH,
  NIDA, NIAAA all have plan priorities AoU is delivering. Strongest WG-1
  evidence outside of genomics.
- *NLM × AoU is mission-explicit*, not just thematic. NLM's 2017–2027
  plan names AoU.

## (B) Where ICs prioritize things AoU is NOT doing — *demand-side gaps*

IC strategic-plan priorities tagged with themes where the AoU corpus
has <5 fractional pubs (or thin per HA detector). These are candidates
for Refresh WG recommendations.

### B1. Environmental health (Theme 19) — across-the-board gap

1. **NIEHS × Environment (Theme 19).** NIEHS has 6 priorities tagged
   to T19 (climate, EJ, exposome, NAMs, EHS workforce, infrastructure).
   AoU corpus: 7 substantive pubs (HA5 detector finds 12 — mostly
   SDOH-environment hybrids). **Largest single demand-supply gap.**
   This is the canonical Refresh gap. Phase 3b notes T19 had zero
   pre-2023 pubs; the area is brand new. If NIEHS is in the alignment
   conversation, AoU needs to materially expand environmental-exposure
   linkages (PM2.5, EJI, heat exposure, water quality).
2. **NHLBI × Environment via crosscutting Lifestyle/Built environment.**
   NHLBI's Crosscutting Lifestyle theme implicitly includes environment;
   AoU's NHLBI-cohort population could deliver geocoded air-quality + CVD
   work that the 7 AoU env pubs barely touch.
3. **NCCIH × Environment via Whole Person Health (NCCIH-O3).** NCCIH
   explicitly puts "environmental" as one of four interconnected domains
   of Whole Person Health; AoU has very limited environmental data
   linkage to support this.

### B2. Imaging / radiomics — structural data gap

4. **NCI × Imaging (Theme 5 + HA3).** NCI Annual Plan FY26's AI-for-
   screening priority (NCIB-AI) implies image-based detection capability.
   AoU corpus: 8 imaging pubs (HA3 detector), nearly all using *external*
   imaging (UK Biobank MRI fat measures). AoU has not yet released
   primary imaging data (chest X-rays, retinal photos, MRI). If the
   2026 Imaging WG is asking "is AoU positioned for imaging-AI work?",
   the answer per the corpus today is no.
5. **NEI × Imaging (Theme 18).** NEI G6 (data science & imaging for
   ophthalmology) — retinal imaging via AoU could enable DR/AMD
   teleophthalmology screening, but AoU doesn't carry retinal images.
6. **NIBIB × Imaging methods (Theme 3+7).** NIBIB Goal 4 (biomedical
   imaging) — same gap. Imaging is an AoU upstream-investment question,
   not a corpus question.

### B3. Multi-omics beyond genomics — partial gap

7. **NIDDK × Multi-omics (HA1).** NIDDK's Research Opportunity 1.3
   includes proteomics/metabolomics linked to phenotypes. AoU HA1
   detector: 24 pubs (thin); only 23 post-2023. Most use *external*
   proteomic data linked to AoU genotypes. The 2026 multi-omics
   release is the supply-side answer but it hasn't flowed through yet.
8. **NHLBI × Multi-omics (HA1).** NHLBI's Objective 4 (individual
   differences in pathobiology) implies multi-omics. Same supply-side
   gap.

### B4. Pharmacogenomics — small footprint vs. multiple IC interest

9. **NIGMS × Pharmacogenomics (Theme 16).** NIGMS PGRN priority
   (NIGMS-G4). AoU corpus: 32 pubs / 200 projects. Small relative
   to NIGMS interest and to AoU's potential — multi-ancestry PGx
   is a natural AoU strength.
10. **NCATS × Drug repurposing (Theme 10+16).** NCATS-AI priority +
    Theme 10/16 thinness in the corpus.

### B5. NAMs / alternative methods

11. **NIEHS × NAMs (NIEHS-T5).** NIEHS prioritizes New Approach
    Methodologies; AoU corpus has effectively zero — but AoU's role
    here is as a human-data source for NAM-validation pipelines,
    which it does not yet do.

### B6. Pregnancy / maternal / women's health — partial gap

12. **NICHD × Pregnancy (Theme 15).** NICHD has 5 plan priorities;
    AoU has 37 pubs / 678 projects. The pub footprint is small; the
    project footprint (5x larger) suggests work is underway but not
    yet flowing to publications. Mid-growth, partial alignment.
13. **NIH-Wide Crosscutting Women's Health × Pregnancy.** Same
    structural under-publication.

### B7. Nutrition / microbiome — borderline gap

14. **NIDDK × Microbiome (Theme 1 / NIDDK-MICRO).** NIDDK priority
    on gut microbiome; HA7 detector: 11 pubs, mostly not primary
    nutrition/microbiome work. AoU does not collect microbiome
    samples — a structural limit.

### B8. Cross-IC specific gaps

15. **NCCIH × Whole Person (Theme 4+6+1+19).** NCCIH's whole-person
    framework asks for integrated bio-behavioral-social-environmental
    research; AoU has the four components separately but few
    integrated pubs. HA6 (G×E×SDOH integrated) had 0 pre-2023, 10
    post-2023 — emerging.

### Headline reads for the Refresh

- *Environment is the single biggest gap-with-named-IC-demand.* If the
  Refresh is going to recommend one new emphasis, environment (with
  data-linkage investment) is the clearest demand-side case.
- *Imaging is a data-supply question, not a research-incentive question.*
  AoU corpus reflects no imaging output because AoU doesn't release
  imaging data. Decision needed: invest upstream or de-emphasize the area.
- *Multi-omics is a 2026-release-dependent question.* The 2026 omics
  release will likely close this gap mechanically; the Refresh should
  flag it as "watch this space" rather than recommend new investment.

## (C) Where AoU researchers produce work that IC plans don't emphasize —
## *latent areas*

Themes with strong corpus activity but minimal IC priority match.

1. **Dermatology (Theme 12).** 197 AoU pubs (14.3% of corpus) is
   anomalously large. NIAMS has the IC home but only 2 of its 4 stated
   priorities touch dermatology, and the NCI cross-attribution is for
   skin cancer only. Phase 1 README flagged this is driven by 1–2
   prolific groups; Phase 3b trend score is −3.9 (declining share).
   **Latent because of an artifact: AoU does not have an underlying
   strategic disease-research push behind it.** The Refresh should not
   treat dermatology as a Refresh priority — but should not ignore that
   it's the area NIAMS most depends on from AoU.
2. **Mental health × physical disease comorbidity (Theme 4 + 1/8/13).**
   Strong AoU corpus presence (e.g., dysphonia + anxiety, sleep + AF,
   loneliness + T2D). NIMH's Comorbidities cross-cutting theme exists
   but is a single cross-cut, not a fleshed-out priority. AoU is
   *ahead* of the IC plan articulation here. The Refresh could
   surface this as a structural strength.
3. **Polygenic-score portability across ancestries (Theme 2).** AoU
   corpus delivers heavy PRS-portability work matching NHGRI Bold
   Prediction #9, but no IC has it as an explicit named strategic
   priority. Likely below-the-radar at the IC plan level relative to
   its corpus footprint.
4. **Wearables ↔ EHR longitudinal phenotyping (Theme 3+7).** AoU's
   EHR-wearable integration capability has 39+ workspaces with
   sustained activity. NIBIB Goal 2 captures the device side but
   the integrated-phenotyping framing is *more* mature in AoU than
   any IC plan articulates.
5. **Subcontinental ancestry variation (Theme 2).** A defining AoU
   contribution (PMID:40480197) but no IC strategic plan in scope
   names subcontinental variation as a priority — only continental.
   AoU is ahead of plan articulation.
6. **Patient-portal / hybrid recruitment methodology (Theme 17).**
   AoU corpus delivers recruitment-methodology work (PMID:38917428)
   that no individual IC plan emphasizes but that nearly every IC
   would benefit from. **A natural pan-NIH-infrastructure framing**:
   one of the things AoU has invented that ICs implicitly need.
7. **CKM syndrome integration (Theme 1.3-CKM subtheme).** NHLBI and
   NIDDK both have it implicit in their plans, but neither has it as
   a primary stated priority. AoU corpus has 2 substantive CKM pubs;
   the *cross-IC framing* is more mature than the IC framings.

### Headline reads for the Refresh

- *Mental-physical comorbidity, polygenic portability, hybrid recruitment
  methods, and CKM-integrated framing* are all areas where AoU is
  intellectually ahead of IC strategic articulation. These belong in the
  WG-1 (Roadmap Refresh) "AoU value-add" framing.
- *Dermatology is an artifact.* Don't elevate it as a Refresh priority
  even though it's a top-3 corpus theme.

## (D) Cross-cutting / pan-NIH priorities — *AoU as pan-NIH infrastructure*

The NIH-Wide Strategic Plan FY2021–2025 has 3 Objectives + 5
Crosscutting Themes. The five Crosscutting Themes are:

1. **Improving Minority Health & Reducing Health Disparities** —
   AoU is structurally aligned (UBR oversampling design). The single
   most defensible "AoU embodies a pan-NIH crosscutting theme" claim.
2. **Enhancing Women's Health** — AoU's pregnancy/maternal +
   gynecologic-condition + women's-health-genetics work is partial
   delivery; the 37 pregnancy pubs are small relative to AoU's
   capacity.
3. **Addressing Public Health Challenges Across the Lifespan** —
   AoU has adolescent (limited), adult (strong), and older-adult
   (growing — NIA-aligned) representation; pediatric is a thin spot.
4. **Promoting Collaborative Science** — AoU is the model
   collaborative-cohort infrastructure (Researcher Workbench, 12K+
   workspaces). Cleanest crosscutting alignment.
5. **Leveraging Data Science for Biomedical Discovery** — AoU is the
   exemplar: 31 AI/foundation-model HA4 pubs, 228 methods/infrastructure
   pubs (T3) growing fast.

### Other plans with explicit pan-NIH-infrastructure relevance

- **NLM** Goal 1 + the AoU-named partnership statement = the most
  explicit IC-level "we depend on AoU" statement in any of the 22
  plans.
- **NHGRI** Bold Predictions #5 and #9 implicitly require pan-IC
  cohort infrastructure (millions of human participants; equitable
  ancestral benefit) — AoU is the pan-IC delivery vehicle for both.
- **NIMHD** Goal 7 Strategy ("analysis of populations experiencing
  health disparities in big data sets ... and future big science
  initiatives") = explicit pan-IC-infrastructure ask that AoU
  delivers on.
- **NIH-Wide Objective 1 — Building Data Resources to Enable Research
  Progress** = AoU is one of the named exemplars.

### Headline reads for the WG-1 / Communications WG

- **The pan-IC infrastructure case is empirically supported in five
  separate plans** (NIH-Wide, NLM, NHGRI, NIMHD, NIH-Wide CCT4/CCT5).
  This is much more than one IC's depend-on statement.
- **All five Crosscutting Themes are AoU strengths.** No IC alone owns
  any of them; AoU is the cross-IC delivery vehicle.
- **Climate/environment is the one crosscut AoU does *not* yet deliver.**
  Worth naming this as an honest gap relative to the IC asks.
