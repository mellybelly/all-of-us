# NASEM strategy & consensus reports

Curated National Academies (NASEM / NAM) reports that bear on the AoU
Scientific Roadmap Refresh, added per the June 3, 2026 WG meeting (**Jordan:
"Add NASEM strategy docs"** — see `../../wg-meeting-notes/2026-06-03_second-meeting.md`).
These sit alongside the NIH IC strategic plans in `../` as a second axis of
strategic context: where the IC plans say what each *Institute* prioritizes,
these say what the *independent scientific consensus* recommends for the
enterprise AoU operates within.

**Selection is curated (Haendel, 2026-06-11) and open to revision** — if Jordan
or the WG has a specific NASEM list in mind, treat this as the starting set.
The reports below were chosen for direct relevance to the 8 focus areas and the
slide-7 "since 2023" priorities (AI, multi-omics, return of results, equity,
data infrastructure).

## How to get the PDFs

National Academies Press PDFs are **free but gated behind a one-click download
form** (direct `curl` returns the HTML landing page, not the PDF). To populate
this folder, open each catalog link below and click "Download Free PDF," then
save into this directory with the suggested filename. Read-online (NCBI
Bookshelf) links are given where available for quick reference without download.
